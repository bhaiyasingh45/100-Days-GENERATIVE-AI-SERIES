## Learning objective
Understand AI as the broad field of building systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, including learning, reasoning, perception, problem-solving, and interaction.

## Instagram caption
🧠 **GENERATIVE AI SERIES #01 — What is Artificial Intelligence?**

Before we understand Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, and AI Agents, we need to understand the foundation — **Artificial Intelligence (AI).**

AI enables machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as:

🔹 Learning from data  
🔹 Reasoning and decision-making  
🔹 Understanding language and patterns  
🔹 Solving complex problems  
🔹 Interacting with humans

From recommendation systems and voice assistants to healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems — **AI is already part of our everyday lives.**

💡 **Key takeaway:**  
AI isn't simply about replacing humans. It's about building systems that can **augment human capabilities and help us solve problems more intelligently.**
