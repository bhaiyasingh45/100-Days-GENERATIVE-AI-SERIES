## Topic

## Learning objective

## Instagram caption
🧠 **GENERATIVE AI SERIES #06 — What Are Foundation Models?**

Before we dive deeper into LLMs, it’s important to understand **Foundation Models** — the powerful base models behind many modern AI applications.

Foundation models are large-scale models trained on broad datasets and designed to support many different tasks and applications.

🔹 Trained on large and diverse datasets  
🔹 Learn general patterns and representations  
🔹 Can support many different tasks  
🔹 Can be adapted through prompting, fine-tuning, RAG, tools, and other techniques  
🔹 Serve as a foundation for many AI applications

Think of a foundation model as a **base layer** that can be adapted for specific use cases.

💡 **Key takeaway:**  
A foundation model is not built for just one narrow task. It provides a general-purpose base that can be adapted into many real-world AI applications.
