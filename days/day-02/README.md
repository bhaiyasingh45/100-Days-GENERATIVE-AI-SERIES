# Day 02 — AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning

## Title
**Generative AI Series #02 — AI vs ML vs DL**

## Topic
Understanding the relationship between Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.

## Learning objective
Understand AI as the broad field, ML as a subset of AI that learns patterns from data, and DL as a subset of ML that uses deep neural networks to learn increasingly complex representations.

## Instagram caption
🧠 **GENERATIVE AI SERIES #02 — AI vs ML vs DL**

AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning are often used interchangeably—but they are not the same thing.

🔹 **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is the broad concept of making machines intelligent.
📊 **Machine Learning (ML)** is a subset of AI that enables machines to learn from data.
🧠 **Deep Learning (DL)** is a subset of ML that uses deep neural networks to learn complex patterns.

Think of it simply:

**AI → the goal**  
**ML → an approach to achieve it**  
**DL → a powerful technique within ML**

💡 **Key takeaway:**  
All Deep Learning is Machine Learning.  
All Machine Learning is Artificial Intelligence.  
**But not all AI is Machine Learning.**
